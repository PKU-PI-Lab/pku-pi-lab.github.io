🔬 Research Directions研究方向

We are a frontier research team co-founded by master’s and PhD researchers from Peking University, Tsinghua University, Stanford University, the University of California, Berkeley, and the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences. Our core members have long cultivated deep expertise in embodied intelligence, world models, and robot learning, and are dedicated to building next-generation intelligent systems for the real physical world.我们是一支由北京大学、清华大学、斯坦福大学、加州大学伯克利分校及中国科学院自动化研究所硕博士研究人员共同组建的前沿科研团队，核心成员长期深耕具身智能、世界模型与机器人学习，致力于构建面向真实物理世界的下一代智能系统。

We focus on developing next-generation embodied intelligent systems for the real physical world. We aim to build embodied world models capable of understanding spatial structures, perceiving physical interactions, predicting environmental dynamics, and sustaining continuous learning through action — endowing robots with authentic perception, reasoning, prediction, and interaction capabilities for the real world.致力于研究面向真实物理世界的下一代具身智能系统。我们希望构建能够理解空间结构、感知物理交互、预测环境变化，并通过行动持续学习的具身世界模型，使机器人真正具备对现实世界的感知、推理、预测与交互能力。

The team centers its research on embodied AI world models and physical intelligence, carrying out cutting-edge work across key directions including geometric representation, tactile and force sensing, 4D spatio-temporal modeling, and physics-grounded representation learning. We are now opening applications for Senior Algorithm Interns: you will work alongside our team to explore high-impact foundational problems in embodied intelligence and physical intelligence, and drive rigorous real-world validation and deployment of research outcomes on actual robotic systems.团队重点关注具身智能世界模型与物理智能，并围绕几何表征、触觉与力觉、4D时空建模、物理表征学习等方向开展前沿研究。现招聘高级算法实习生，与团队共同探索具身智能与物理智能领域的重要问题，并推动研究成果在真实机器人系统中的验证与落地。

🔬 Research研究方向

Embodied AI Models & Robot Learning具身智能模型与机器人学习

Multimodal embodied foundation models (VLA / world models / PAM), imitation and reinforcement learning for contact-rich manipulation, sim-to-real transfer, and reliable long-horizon task planning through physics-semantics-driven agent clusters (PySAC).多模态具身基础模型（VLA / 世界模型 / PAM）、面向富接触操作的模仿与强化学习、仿真到真实的迁移，以及通过物理语义驱动的智能体集群（PySAC）实现可靠的长程任务规划。

Physics-Informed Machine Learning物理信息机器学习

Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs), neural operators for large-scale multi-scale PDE solving, and mechanism–data fusion models that bring physical semantics into learning-based control.物理信息神经网络（PINNs）、面向大规模多尺度 PDE 求解的神经算子，以及将物理语义引入学习型控制的机理–数据融合模型。

Cloud-Fog Automation & Industrial eCPS云雾自动化与工业信息物理系统

Cloud-Fog Automation (CFA): software-defined, intelligence-native industrial control infrastructure with communication–computing–control co-design, virtualized controllers, converged 5G/6G networks, and functional safety.云雾自动化（CFA）：智能原生的软件定义工业控制基础设施，通信–计算–控制协同设计、虚拟化控制器、融合的 5G/6G 网络与功能安全。

Robot Systems & Industrial Deployment机器人系统与工业部署

High-dynamic dexterous robotic systems, dexterous hands, embodied toolchains (ETC), embodied model libraries (EML), and edge controllers (ECC) — deployed as reusable process packages for flexible precision manufacturing in semiconductor, aerospace, rail-transit, and new-energy equipment.高动态灵巧机器人系统、灵巧手、具身工具链（ETC）、具身模型库（EML）与边缘控制器（ECC）——作为可复用的工艺包部署于半导体、航空航天、轨道交通与新能源装备的柔性精密制造。
