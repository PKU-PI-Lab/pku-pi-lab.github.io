About the Lab

The Physical Intelligence Lab (物理智能实验室) at Peking University, led by Prof. Zhibo Pang, focuses on robots and autonomous control systems for both traditional and emerging industrial scenarios. We develop the full stack of urgently needed intelligent software and hardware technologies — from multimodal embodied AI models to high-dynamic dexterous robotic systems — with the goal of redefining flexible precision manufacturing through embodied intelligence.

Our research is organized around the PAM (Physical-Attribute Model) paradigm: a unified embodied-intelligence framework that represents the physical semantics affecting robot action generation, efficiently produces massive simulation scenarios and training data, and delivers generalization, precision, and industrial-grade reliability for high-value contact-rich manipulation tasks. We pursue a deep integration of mechanics, classical control, and artificial intelligence.

Prof. Pang proposed the Cloud-Fog Automation (CFA) and Cloud-Fog Robotics technology frameworks in 2017, which were later highlighted by ABB's global CTO and adopted in ABB's product strategy, including the OmniCore robot controller generation. Before joining Peking University, he spent twelve years at ABB Corporate Research Sweden, where he led the software and communication technologies of several world-first industrial products, including ABB Mobile YuMi — the industry's first mobile dual-arm humanoid collaborative robot.

🔬 Research

Embodied AI Models & Robot Learning

Multimodal embodied foundation models (VLA / world models / PAM), imitation and reinforcement learning for contact-rich manipulation, sim-to-real transfer, and reliable long-horizon task planning through physics-semantics-driven agent clusters (PySAC).

Physics-Informed Machine Learning

Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs), neural operators for large-scale multi-scale PDE solving, and mechanism–data fusion models that bring physical semantics into learning-based control.

Cloud-Fog Automation & Industrial eCPS

Cloud-Fog Automation (CFA): software-defined, intelligence-native industrial control infrastructure with communication–computing–control co-design, virtualized controllers, converged 5G/6G networks, and functional safety.

Robot Systems & Industrial Deployment

High-dynamic dexterous robotic systems, dexterous hands, embodied toolchains (ETC), embodied model libraries (EML), and edge controllers (ECC) — deployed as reusable process packages for flexible precision manufacturing in semiconductor, aerospace, rail-transit, and new-energy equipment.
