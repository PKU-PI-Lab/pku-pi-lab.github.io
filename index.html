🔬 Research Directions研究方向

His current research interests include multimodal physical intelligence models (RSI/VLM/VLA/WAM, etc.), efficient physical AI, safe physical AI, robotic systems, dexterous hands, AI for PDEs, physics-informed neural networks (PINNs), high-computing-power controllers, and integrated cloud-computing and control, among others.他目前的研究兴趣包括，多模态物理智能模型（RSI/VLM/VLA/WAM等）、高效物理AI、安全物理AI、机器人系统、灵巧手、AI4PDE、物理信息神经网路（PINNs）、高算力控制器、网算控一体化等。

🔬 Research研究方向

Embodied AI Models & Robot Learning具身智能模型与机器人学习

Multimodal embodied foundation models (VLA / world models / PAM), imitation and reinforcement learning for contact-rich manipulation, sim-to-real transfer, and reliable long-horizon task planning through physics-semantics-driven agent clusters (PySAC).多模态具身基础模型（VLA / 世界模型 / PAM）、面向富接触操作的模仿与强化学习、仿真到真实的迁移，以及通过物理语义驱动的智能体集群（PySAC）实现可靠的长程任务规划。

Physics-Informed Machine Learning物理信息机器学习

Physics-informed neural networks (PINNs), neural operators for large-scale multi-scale PDE solving, and mechanism–data fusion models that bring physical semantics into learning-based control.物理信息神经网络（PINNs）、面向大规模多尺度 PDE 求解的神经算子，以及将物理语义引入学习型控制的机理–数据融合模型。

Cloud-Fog Automation & Industrial eCPS云雾自动化与工业信息物理系统

Cloud-Fog Automation (CFA): software-defined, intelligence-native industrial control infrastructure with communication–computing–control co-design, virtualized controllers, converged 5G/6G networks, and functional safety.云雾自动化（CFA）：智能原生的软件定义工业控制基础设施，通信–计算–控制协同设计、虚拟化控制器、融合的 5G/6G 网络与功能安全。

Robot Systems & Industrial Deployment机器人系统与工业部署

High-dynamic dexterous robotic systems, dexterous hands, embodied toolchains (ETC), embodied model libraries (EML), and edge controllers (ECC) — deployed as reusable process packages for flexible precision manufacturing in semiconductor, aerospace, rail-transit, and new-energy equipment.高动态灵巧机器人系统、灵巧手、具身工具链（ETC）、具身模型库（EML）与边缘控制器（ECC）——作为可复用的工艺包部署于半导体、航空航天、轨道交通与新能源装备的柔性精密制造。

📚 Selected Publications部分论文

* Corresponding author. Full and up-to-date list available upon request.* 通讯作者。完整最新列表可来函索取。

Y. Lou et al. "Dynamicwam: Dual-path motion conditioning for world-action models in dynamic manipulation." arXiv preprint arXiv:2608.00793 (2026).

PaperCode

Proposes DynamicWAM, a dual-path motion conditioning framework integrating historical optical flow, kinematic features, and asynchronous execution, achieving a 38.2% success rate and a 53.2 manipulation score on DOMINO, while improving real-world manipulation success by 22.9 percentage points over the strongest baseline to 46.7%.提出 DynamicWAM 双路径运动条件融合技术，通过结合历史光流、运动学特征与异步执行机制，在 DOMINO 基准上实现 38.2% 的任务成功率和 53.2 的操作评分，真实环境下平均成功率达到 46.7%，较最强基线提升 22.9 个百分点。

M. Li et al. "PS-DLO: Property-and Spatially Aware Deformable Linear Object Manipulation Policy." arXiv preprint arXiv:2610.34147 (2026).

PaperCode

Proposes PS-DLO, a property- and spatially aware manipulation policy integrating contrastive image-text learning for physical property priors and SAM3-based future spatial feature distillation, achieving an average success rate of 68.9% across nine real-world deformable linear object manipulation tasks, outperforming the strongest baseline by 20.0 percentage points.提出 PS-DLO 物理属性与空间感知融合策略，通过结合对比式图文学习提取物体属性先验，并利用 SAM3 进行未来空间特征蒸馏，在 9 项真实机器人柔性线状物体操作任务中实现 68.9% 的平均成功率，较最强基线提升 20.0 个百分点。

X. Han et al. "ForceBench: Benchmarking Force Integration for Robotic Manipulation." arXiv preprint arXiv:2610.19604 (2026).

PaperCode

Introduces ForceBench, a unified benchmark for evaluating six-axis wrist-force feedback integration, demonstrating that Prompt Fusion improves the average robotic manipulation success rate from 23.25% to 35.31% in simulation and from 40.00% to 63.00% in real-world tasks.提出 ForceBench 力觉融合评测基准，通过对比六种六轴腕部力反馈融合技术，发现 Prompt Fusion 方法将机器人操作任务的平均成功率从 23.25% 提升至 35.31%（仿真），真实环境下从 40.00% 提升至 63.00%。

Y. Fu et al., "CableSort: Force-Guided Dual-Arm Sorting of Stacked Cables," RSS 2026 DAROMA Workshop (2026).

Paper

Proposes CableSort, a force-guided dual-arm cable sorting framework integrating structure-aware instance segmentation, eagle-claw grippers, and adaptive bilateral wrist-force feedback, achieving 0.93 instance segmentation IoU and a 48.0% end-to-end task success rate, outperforming the fixed-distance control baseline by 22.0 percentage points in simulation.提出 CableSort 力觉引导双臂线缆分拣框架，通过融合结构感知实例分割、鹰爪式夹爪与双腕力突变自适应控制技术，实现 0.93 的实例分割 IoU 和 48.0% 的端到端任务成功率，较固定距离控制基线提升 22.0 个百分点（仿真环境）。

Y. Luo et al., "MMET: A Multi-Input and Multi-Scale Transformer for Efficient PDEs Solving," Proceedings of the 34th International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence (IJCAI), 2025.

PaperCode

Proposes MMET, a multi-input and multi-scale efficient Transformer framework integrating gated condition embedding, encoder-decoder decoupling, and Hilbert curve-based reserialization for efficient PDE solving, reducing the relative L2 error in displacement prediction by approximately 86.2%, from 0.337 to 0.0464, on the Beam2d benchmark.提出 MMET 多输入多尺度高效 Transformer 框架，通过融合门控条件嵌入、编码器-解码器解耦及 Hilbert 曲线重序列化技术，实现高效偏微分方程求解，在 Beam2d 基准上将位移预测的相对 L2 误差从 0.337 降至 0.0464，降低约 86.2%。

Y. Luo et al., "Mitigating Gradient Pathology in PINNs through Aligned Constraint", Proceedings of the 43rd International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML), 2026.

PaperCode

Proposes CAML (Constraint-Aligned Loss with Manifold Lifting), integrating zeroth-order constraint reformulation and delayed residual optimization to mitigate gradient conflicts in PINNs, increasing the fraction of training steps with aligned gradients from 5.89% to 39.16% on the Heat benchmark with an MLP backbone, while significantly improving convergence stability and efficiency for PDE solving.提出 CAML（约束对齐与流形提升）技术，通过零阶项约束重构与残差损失延迟机制缓解物理信息神经网络（PINNs）的梯度冲突，在 Heat 基准的 MLP 架构上将梯度方向一致的训练步数占比从 5.89% 提升至 39.16%，显著改善 PDE 求解的收敛稳定性与效率。

D. Hu et al., "Spectral-Temporal Conditioning: A Frequency-Domain Framework for Robust Robotic Imitation Learning" IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics (SCI-Q1).

Paper

Proposes Temp-ACT, a frequency-domain imitation learning framework integrating historical trajectory conditioning via STFT, asymmetric sinusoidal windowing, and learnable low-pass filtering to mitigate trajectory oscillations and cyclic stagnation in precision robotic manipulation, improving full-task success rates from 3% to 36% in simulation and from 5% to 30% in real-world experiments, representing gains of 33 and 25 percentage points, respectively.提出 Temp-ACT 频域增强模仿学习框架，通过融合历史轨迹短时傅里叶变换（STFT）、非对称正弦窗及可学习低通滤波技术，缓解机器人精密操作中的轨迹振荡与循环停滞问题，将端到端任务成功率从 3% 提升至 36%（仿真），从 5% 提升至 30%（真实环境），分别提高 33 和 25 个百分点。

F. Ma et al., "Action prompt integration in large language models for time series forecasting of nuclear power industry systems," Advanced Engineering Informatics (SCI-Q1).

Paper

Proposes APiLLM, an action-prompt-enhanced LLM framework integrating adaptive trend-seasonality-residual decomposition, dynamic prompt-pool retrieval, and action-aware attention bias for industrial time-series forecasting, reducing prediction MSE by approximately 45.8%, from 0.2317 to 0.1256, compared with GPT4TS in the nuclear power circulating water system pipeline-leakage scenario.提出 APiLLM 动作提示增强大语言模型时序预测框架，通过融合趋势-季节性-残差自适应分解、动态提示池检索与动作感知注意力偏置技术，提升工业系统在操作干预和异常工况下的预测精度，在核电循环水系统管道泄漏场景中将预测均方误差（MSE）从 0.2317 降至 0.1256，较 GPT4TS 基线降低约 45.8%。

F. Ma et al., "Embodied Artificial Intelligence-Driven Integrated Development Environment for Industrial Automation Systems: Requirements and Framework," IEEE Industrial Electronics Magazine (SCI-Q2).

Paper

Proposes a modular IDE framework for embodied AI in industrial automation, integrating enhanced functional modules, lifecycle-oriented workflow management, and hardware-agnostic deployment to improve system deployability, maintainability, interoperability, and trustworthiness.提出面向工业自动化具身智能的模块化集成开发环境（IDE）概念框架，通过融合功能模块增强、全生命周期工作流管理及硬件无关部署技术，提升具身智能系统的可部署性、可维护性、互操作性与安全可信性。
